- 3*3 grid
- 2 player game
- Each player selects a character before the game i.e. 'X' OR 'O'
- Player plays by placing the character on the grid 
- A player wins by placing three of its character in a row or column or diagonal 
- Game ends when a player wins or all grids cells are filled - resulting in a draw


Objects involved : 
- Playing character
- Board - n*n
- Player
